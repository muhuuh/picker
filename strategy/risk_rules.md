# Risk Rules

Last updated: 2026-04-30

## Purpose

This file defines risk rules for research, alerts, and human review.

## Always Flag

- Material filing changes.
- Guidance changes.
- Auditor, accounting, or internal-control issues.
- Major debt, liquidity, dilution, or covenant issues.
- Regulatory or legal developments.
- Management turnover.
- Sharp thesis contradiction versus the current company file.
- Social-media hype unsupported by filings, financials, or credible reporting.

## Human Approval Required

- Buy, sell, or position-size recommendations.
- Moving a stock between current holdings, monitoring, and rejected.
- Overriding the 6-week rejected-stock cooldown.
- Major strategy changes.

## Evidence Rules

- Preserve conflicting evidence instead of hiding it.
- Mark unknown metrics as unknown; do not guess.
- Label social sentiment as sentiment, not fact.
- Include source links or internal run artifact references for material claims.
