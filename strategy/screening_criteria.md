## Minimum Evidence Before Alerting

- Company identity and ticker verified.
- At least one credible source explaining why the company is relevant.
- Clear link to a strategy theme or screening criterion.
- No active 6-week rejected-stock cooldown.

## Candidate Priority Levels

- `low`: worth saving, no urgent follow-up.
- `medium`: deserves research in the next weekly run.
- `high`: strong strategy fit or meaningful new development.
- `urgent`: material event affecting a current holding or unusually strong candidate signal.
