## Thesis

- Why this company is interesting: LPKF owns differentiated laser and LIDE glass-processing technology that could benefit if glass substrates, through-glass vias, and co-packaged optics become more important in AI/HPC advanced packaging.
- What must be true: semiconductor customers must move from R&D and qualification toward production systems, LPKF must protect LIDE's technical edge, and the North Star cost program must bridge the company through weak Solar demand.
- Main upside drivers: initial production-equipment orders for Advanced Semiconductor Packaging, higher order intake, the North Star restructuring program, and longer-term perovskite/solar optionality.
- Main downside risks: no confirmed series-production order yet, weak Solar segment, China/geopolitical pressure, small-cap execution risk, and valuation sensitivity after a sharp social-media-driven rerating.
