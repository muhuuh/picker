## Follow-up Research Notes

### 2026-05-17 User Follow-up Research

- The NVIDIA relationship is a real AI-infrastructure validation event, but should not be overstated as proof that the full >5 GW pipeline is already contracted or revenue-producing.
- Fresh Exa/company sources describe a large managed GPU compute agreement using NVIDIA Blackwell GPUs, deployment within existing 60 MW Childress data centers, and a ramp expected from early 2027.
- NVIDIA also has a five-year right/warrant to purchase up to 30M IREN shares at $70/share, subject to conditions and approvals. This validates alignment but is not immediate cash unless exercised.
- Revenue decline is a yellow flag, not automatically a red flag. It appears tied to the transition from Bitcoin mining toward AI cloud, including lower mining contribution, decommissioning/redeployment of hardware, GPU installation timing, and billing ramp timing.
- Debt/convertibles fund both confirmed buildout and anticipated future AI demand. Treat "new deal coming soon" chatter as a Grok/X social signal until company filings or announcements verify it.
- Sweetwater and the >5 GW pipeline are valuable because AI data centers are power/land constrained. The gating issue is actual energized MW, GPU deployment timing, customer contracts, and revenue per MW versus competitors.
