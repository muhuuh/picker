# Market Research

This folder stores broader research that is not tied to a single company file.

## Folders

- `industries/`: industry-level research, competitive shifts, regulation, and sector mood.
- `themes/`: cross-industry themes and investment narratives.
- `macro/`: macro, rates, inflation, currency, policy, and regional context.
- `discovery/`: new candidate stocks and watchlist ideas surfaced by research.

## Rules

- Cite sources for material claims.
- Keep discovery candidates linked to the strategy or screening criteria.
- Move company-specific deep research into `stock_tracking/stock_info_files/` once a company becomes monitored or held.
- Keep high-level implications for holdings in the relevant category state file.
