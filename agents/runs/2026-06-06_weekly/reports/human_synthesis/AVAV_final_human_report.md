## What AeroVironment Inc. Actually Does

AeroVironment (AVAV) designs, develops, produces, and supports small/medium unmanned aircraft systems (UAS), loitering munitions (e.g., Switchblade), counter-UAS systems, and-following the May 2025 all-stock acquisition of BlueHalo (≈$4.1B enterprise value)-space-based platforms, directed energy, cyber, and electronic warfare capabilities. It operates in two segments: Autonomous Systems (AxS, legacy UAS/loitering munitions focus) and Space, Cyber and Directed Energy (SCDE, post-acquisition diversification).

The global UAV/drone market is expanding rapidly (CAGR estimates 14-18% through 2030-2035), driven by defense demand for ISR, precision strike, and counter-UAS amid geopolitical tensions; defense UAS spending remains a key tailwind. Key competitors include General Atomics (larger Reaper-class), Northrop Grumman, Lockheed Martin, Textron, Kratos (KTOS, smaller tactical), Elbit Systems, and commercial players like DJI (less relevant for U.S. defense).
