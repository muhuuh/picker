## What AXT Inc. Actually Does

- AXT develops and manufactures high-performance compound and single-element semiconductor wafer substrates (primarily InP, GaAs, and Ge) used where silicon cannot meet performance needs for optoelectronic and RF devices. - Core products include InP substrates (key for high-speed optical transceivers in data centers/AI networking), semi-insulating and semi-conducting GaAs (for wireless, LEDs, VCSELs, sensors), and Ge (for satellite solar cells, IR detectors).

- Compound semiconductor substrates face strong multi-year tailwinds from AI data-center optical interconnect demand (InP critical for high-speed photonics) plus 5G/PON expansion, with industry revenue growth forecasts around 21% p.a. versus AXT's higher projected rates. - Key competitors include Sumitomo Electric (Japan), JX Nippon, Freiberger Compound Materials (Germany), Umicore, and Chinese players like CCTC/Vital Materials; AXT differentiates on vertical integration and cost but lags in scale/technology breadth.
