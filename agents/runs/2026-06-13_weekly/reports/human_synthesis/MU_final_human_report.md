## What Micron Technology Actually Does

Micron Technology sits in **Technology / Semiconductors**. Micron Technology designs, manufactures, and sells high-performance memory and storage solutions, primarily DRAM (including high-bandwidth memory or HBM), NAND flash, and related products under the Micron and Crucial brands. These enable data processing and storage across AI, data centers, client/mobile devices, automotive, and industrial applications. Core products/technology include leading-edge DRAM and HBM optimized for AI accelerators (e.g., GPUs), advanced NAND for SSDs, and managed NAND/multichip packages. The company emphasizes technology leadership in density, speed, power efficiency, and yield.

Market context: The memory industry (DRAM/NAND/HBM) is in a strong upcycle through 2026-2027, driven by AI infrastructure buildout creating tight supply, especially in HBM. Hyperscale capex and GPU cluster demand are expected to sustain elevated pricing and utilization into at least 2027, with potential for structural shifts beyond typical cyclical peaks. Key competitors: Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix (the "Big Three" control nearly all advanced memory). SK Hynix leads HBM (~57-62% share in recent quarters), followed by Micron (~21%) and Samsung (~17-22%); The investor question is whether the recent evidence improves the company-specific path through that market, not whether the broad theme is attractive in isolation.
