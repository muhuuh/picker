## What AXT Actually Does

AXT sits in **Technology / SEMICONDUCTOR EQUIPMENT & MATERIALS**. - AXT, Inc. (AXTI) is a materials science company that develops and manufactures high-performance compound and single-element semiconductor wafer substrates (InP, GaAs, and Ge) used when silicon wafers cannot meet performance needs for optoelectronic or high-speed devices. - Core products include indium phosphide (InP) substrates for data center connectivity/silicon photonics, high-speed optical transmission, 5G infrastructure, fiber optic lasers/detectors, PON, and photonic ICs; semi-insulating and semi-conducting GaAs for Wi-Fi/IoT, power amplifiers, LEDs/micro-LEDs, VCSELs (3D sensing, data comms), lasers, and sensors; and germanium (Ge).

Market context: - Compound semiconductor substrates serve niche high-performance applications (optoelectronics, RF, photonics) where silicon falls short; demand tied to 5G rollout, data center optical interconnects (AI-driven), LEDs, and specialty solar/sensors. Industry health over next 1-2 years appears supported by AI/data center optical needs but remains cyclical and exposed to macro/tech spending. - Key competitors: Sumitomo Electric, JX (Japan Energy), Freiberger Compound Materials, Umicore, China Crystal Technology (CCTC), and Vital Materials. The investor question is whether the recent evidence improves the company-specific path through that market, not whether the broad theme is attractive in isolation.
