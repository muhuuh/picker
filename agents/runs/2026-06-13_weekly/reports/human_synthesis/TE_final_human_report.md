## What T1 Energy Actually Does

T1 Energy sits in **Industrials / Electrical Equipment & Parts**. - Manufactures and sells photovoltaic (PV) solar modules using Passivated Emitter and Rear Contact (PERC) and Tunnel Oxide Passivated Contact (TOPCon) technologies at its G1_Dallas facility (Wilmer, Texas; ~5 GW annual module capacity); this is its primary revenue driver, selling to utility-scale developers, commercial & industrial (C&I), and residential customers in the U.S. and select international markets. - Constructing G2_Austin (Texas) solar cell fab (Phase 1 ~2.1 GW targeted for late 2026) to vertically integrate U.S. supply chain and reduce reliance on imports.
