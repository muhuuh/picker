## What T1 Energy Actually Does

T1 Energy is tracked here as a Electrical Equipment & Parts exposure. T1 Energy Inc. (NYSE: TE; formerly FREYR Battery, Inc., name change February 2025) manufactures and sells photovoltaic solar modules and cells, focusing on building an integrated U.S. supply chain for solar and batteries. Its core asset is the advanced G1_Dallas solar module manufacturing facility, one of the world's most advanced, capable of delivering domestic content with industry-leading P-type and N-type technologies at scale-the only U.S. company positioned to do so. It sources cells internationally while advancing vertical integration

Market context: U.S. solar manufacturing is expanding rapidly due to IRA incentives, domestic content premiums, and supply-chain security priorities, with expected continued growth in demand over 1-2 years amid data center/hyperscaler power needs and utility-scale projects. Key tailwinds include potential Section 232 tariffs on foreign polysilicon and safe-harboring activity. Competitors include other U.S. or North American solar module makers (e.g., First Solar for thin-film; emerging crystalline players) and Asian imports facing tariffs.
