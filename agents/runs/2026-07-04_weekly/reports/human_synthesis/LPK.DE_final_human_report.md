## What LPKF Laser & Electronics Actually Does

LPKF Laser & Electronics is tracked here as a Industrial - Machinery exposure. LPKF Laser & Electronics SE develops, manufactures, and sells laser-based precision manufacturing solutions for electronics, semiconductors, automotive, solar, medical, and research markets. It operates via four segments: Development (rapid PCB prototyping systems for R&D, universities, and industry labs), Electronics (PCB production tools including stencil lasers, depaneling, and LIDE/Laser Induced Deep Etching for high-precision glass structuring and components), Welding (laser plastic welding systems for consumer electronics, automotive, and medical parts), and Solar
