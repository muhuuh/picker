The best social insight is the debate over structural change. Investors are no longer asking whether Micron benefits from AI. They are asking whether AI makes the memory cycle less cyclical.

## Bull Case

The bull case is that Micron has moved from commodity DRAM exposure to a strategic AI bandwidth supplier.

What would support that:

- HBM remains supply constrained into 2026 and beyond.
- Gross margin keeps improving with mix.
- AI server demand absorbs new supply.
- Long-term contracts reduce pricing volatility.
- EPS and free cash flow estimates keep rising.

## Bear Case

The bear case is that this is still memory.

Main risks:

- HBM supply catches up by 2027.
- Hyperscaler AI capex slows.
- DRAM/NAND pricing rolls over.
- Geopolitical or tariff shocks hit supply chains.
- Investors extrapolate peak margins too far.
