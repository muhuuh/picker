## What Sivers Actually Does

Sivers Semiconductors AB develops and sells semiconductor chips, modules, and subsystems across two main business areas: Wireless and Photonics.

Wireless includes mmWave beamforming ICs, transceivers, RFICs, antenna modules, SATCOM, fixed wireless access, defense communications/radar-like applications, drone connectivity, and 5G/6G-related systems. Photonics includes indium phosphide lasers, laser arrays, optical amplifiers, and components used in AI datacenter optical interconnects and sensing.

The reason Sivers is interesting is not one isolated product. It is the possibility that the same technical base can show up in several high-value markets:

- AI datacenter optical I/O, co-packaged optics, external light sources, and 1.6T module ecosystems.
- SATCOM, defense communications, and electronically steered antennas.
- Fixed wireless access and 5G/6G mmWave modules.
- LiDAR and remote sensing.
- Western/European indium phosphide supply-chain optionality through the Glasgow fab narrative.

The strategic strength is breadth. The underwriting problem is also breadth: many opportunities can remain in development, NRE, trials, or pipeline language without turning into repeatable, high-margin product revenue.

Market context from Grok web and the opportunity assessment:

- AI datacenter interconnect demand is pushing toward optical I/O and CPO as copper faces power, latency, bandwidth, and heat limits.
- Sivers competes or overlaps with much larger players including Broadcom, Marvell, Analog Devices, Lumentum, MACOM, and other RF/photonics suppliers.
- Sivers' potential advantage is niche integration and specialized photonics/mmWave capability.
- Its disadvantage is scale, brand power, R&D budget, customer leverage, and lack of clean profitability.
- The industry backdrop is favorable, but customer qualification and volume ramps can take longer than public-market narratives expect.

This is a platform optionality thesis, not a simple earnings-quality thesis.

- Restatements and delayed Q1 reporting create reporting-quality risk.
- 2025 EBIT and net result were deeply negative.
- Gross profit/margin quality remains a key open question.
- Directed issue dilution confirms external financing need.
